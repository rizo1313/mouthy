## ✨ What mouthy Does

Most dictation tools send your voice to a company's servers, transcribe it there, and send the text back. That means your words leave your computer, and it means the app stops working when your internet does.

mouthy works differently. The speech recognition happens inside your computer, using a model that is already stored on your machine. Your voice is converted to text locally, in real time, and the text is inserted into whatever window you are typing in. Nothing is uploaded. Nothing is stored anywhere but on your own hard drive.

The result is a typing tool that is faster than your keyboard for long sentences, that works on an airplane or in a basement with no signal, and that keeps private conversations private.

## 🎯 How to Use mouthy

Once mouthy is running, using it is simple.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

**mouthy does not hear anything when I speak.**
Open Windows Settings, go to Privacy, then Microphone, and confirm that microphone access is turned on for desktop apps. Also check that the correct microphone is selected — if you have a headset plugged in, Windows may still be listening to your laptop's built-in mic.

**The text is accurate but appears in the wrong place.**
mouthy types wherever your cursor currently is. Before you start speaking, click once inside the text field where you want the words to land.

**The app opens but the window is blank or frozen.**
Close mouthy completely, then open it again. If the problem continues, restart your computer and try once more.

**The first transcription is slow.**
The very first use loads the speech model into memory, which takes longer than usual. Later dictations are much faster.

**The download will not start.**
Make sure you are on the official releases page linked above, then try a different browser or temporarily pause any download-blocking extension.

**The text has many errors.**
Speak slightly more slowly, reduce background noise, and move the microphone closer to your mouth. A headset microphone usually improves accuracy dramatically.
